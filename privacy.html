How this site
handles visits.

This site uses Umami Cloud analytics to understand general website usage and when the resume download button is used.

No visitor names, email addresses, or resume-downloader identities are collected. Analytics do not use tracking for advertising or sell visitor data.

GitHub Pages may also process normal technical information, such as IP addresses, as part of hosting and serving the site. The analytics provider may record limited aggregate usage information such as page views, browser type, approximate region, referrer, and resume-download events.
